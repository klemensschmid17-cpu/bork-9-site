FAQ

A few plain answers to keep the project understandable from the first click.

Is BORK-9 affiliated with Dogecoin, SpaceX, or Elon Musk?

No. BORK-9 is an original, unaffiliated dog-and-space parody project. It does not use their logos, people, or company branding.

Can I earn or claim tokens by playing?

Not in this version. Bork Points are local score only. Any future reward season would need published rules, a capped budget, wallet eligibility controls, and a public transaction report before claims open.

Is the BORK9 token already available?

No. This site does not sell a token, connect a wallet, or ask visitors to buy anything. The proposed allocation is shown for transparency only.

What needs to be shown before a launch?

At minimum: exact supply, revocation of mint and freeze authorities, labelled allocation wallets, the 20% unlocked creator allocation, the 0% fee policy, and liquidity / pool evidence.
